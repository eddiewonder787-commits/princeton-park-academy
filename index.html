Academic Programmes

From Early Childhood through to Grade 12 — every stage of your child's education is covered under one roof at Princeton Park Academy.

Early Childhood Education

A nurturing foundation for our youngest learners — building confidence, curiosity, and a love for learning from the very start.

📚
Primary Education

A strong academic foundation across all core subjects with special attention given to literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking skills.
